ABOUT AGIPAREL

Make factory work easier to understand.

Garment manufacturing involves hundreds of handoffs between people, materials, machines and records. Agiparel is being built to bring those pieces together, so teams can see what is happening and decide what to do next.

01 · START WITH REALITY

Understand the factory

Bring together buyer orders, available materials, machines, workforce information and existing documents—without assuming every record is already complete.

02 · FOLLOW THE WORK

Connect each handoff

Link physical materials, cutting, sewing, quality checks and packing to the evidence and people responsible for each step.

03 · LEARN FROM TIME

See delays and variation

Use observed events and timestamps to understand waiting, progress and bottlenecks instead of relying only on estimates.

Where we are today. Agiparel is an evolving factory software platform. The demonstration illustrates its workflows and controls. Actual reader integrations, physical verification, shipment authority and customer deployments require factory-specific validation and permissions.
