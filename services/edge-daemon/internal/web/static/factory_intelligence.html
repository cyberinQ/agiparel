These five areas follow our factory reporting rules. Only values supported by the current source appear.

1. Flow time and slow steps

Typical and slow-end times describe bounded elapsed gaps from saved practice events. A missing or overnight gap is unknown, not a long wait or zero. We need at least five valid samples before comparing slow-end times.

3. Quality and repair

Current QC accepted, held and pending counts appear above and in the source table. First-pass yield, defects per hundred and repair turnaround are not measured by this report because their first-inspection and lifecycle evidence is not reconstructed.

4. Fabric and cutting

Cut marker positions and draft bundle candidates are not verified fabric consumption or accepted cut garments. Fabric yield, remnant value and cut-to-ship conservation are not measured here.

Check Fabric Store and Cutting for their source records.

5. Shipment, cost and margin

Packing and dispatch rehearsal events are not buyer-accepted shipments. Real OTIF, labor cost, cost per accepted garment and order margin are not measured. These require approved commercial and actual-cost records.

See Packing for the practice packing records.
