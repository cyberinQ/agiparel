OPTIONAL READINGWhy Agiparel?Three questions about the factory we are building+

1. What are we trying to do?

Make garment factories easier to run. Give managers and workers one reliable picture of every order, material, garment, task, problem and delivery.

Help factories make the right garments, at the right cost and on time—without losing materials, repeating work or relying on incomplete information.

Know what the factory has, what it needs to make, what is happening, what is going wrong, and what to do next.

2. How is it typically done today—and what are the limits?

Many factories piece together spreadsheets, paper, messages, separate software and supervisors’ experience. Buyer orders, fabric stock, cutting, sewing, quality and shipping records often live in different places.

That makes it hard to locate delays, reconcile quantities, confirm real progress or make timely decisions. A recorded transaction does not always establish what happened to a physical garment.

3. What is different about Agiparel?

Agiparel connects documents, materials, physical movement, human decisions and business rules in one continuous record:

  1. Start with the real factory. Work from its existing processes and improve incomplete records as you go.
  2. Follow the physical work. Connect fabric rolls, cut parts, reusable RFID carriers, garments and cartons.
  3. Verify consequential actions. Require appropriate evidence and authorization before important records change.
  4. Find the lost time. Use actual timestamps to measure waiting, process time and variability.
  5. Help people decide. Make gaps and alternatives visible without presenting guesses as facts.

Physical readers, real-world handoffs and shipment authority require factory-specific commissioning and approval; software demos do not establish those facts.
